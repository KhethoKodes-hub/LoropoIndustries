Our Journey

14 YEARS.
ONE
MISSION.

From a single contract in Kathu to a R2.4 billion pipeline, Loropo has grown by doing one thing consistently — showing up and delivering.

2008
Founded — Kathu, Northern Cape
Loropo Industries established as a 100% black-owned enterprise, winning its first contract for earthworks and civil support at an iron ore operation near Sishen.
Founding Year
2012
First Major Kumba Contract
Secured materials handling and waste removal at Kumba Iron Ore's Sishen operation — the beginning of a long-term client relationship that continues today.
Milestone
2016
ISO 45001 & 1M LTI-Free Hours
Achieved international safety certification and crossed the 1 million LTI-free hours milestone across all active operations in the Northern Cape basin.
Safety Landmark
2020
Level 1 B-BBEE & Workforce Expansion
Attained Level 1 B-BBEE contributor status and grew the workforce to 650 employees, with 43% women in management roles — genuine transformation.
B-BBEE Level 1
2025
Best Safety Performer — Kumba Awards
Recognised by Kumba Iron Ore as the best-performing contractor for safety across all Sishen operations. 5 million LTI-free hours achieved. Zero fatalities, all time.
